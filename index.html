About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the FSL (Future Space Lab) at KAIST, South Korea, advised by Prof. Seung Hyun Cha. I received my M.S. from the KAIST Graduate School of Metaverse and my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Seoul National University of Science and Technology.

My research focuses on smart home agents that understand what residents are doing and act on their behalf, aiming for homes that are both reactive, responding reliably when asked, and proactive, anticipating needs before residents ask.
